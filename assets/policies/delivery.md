# Delivery

Maple Market ships kitchen, pantry, and home goods from our neighbourhood shop.

## Timing

Standard delivery is **3–5 business days after an order ships** (status becomes **shipped**).
Time in **placed** or **processing** does not count toward that window.

**Business days** are Monday–Saturday. We do **not** deliver on Sundays or public holidays.
Orders that would otherwise arrive on a Sunday are delivered the next business day.

We do not offer next-day or timed-slot delivery in this version of the shop.

## Tracking

Tracking appears after the order status becomes **shipped**. There is no tracking number
while the order is still **placed** or **processing**.

If a parcel shows as delivered but you do not have it, wait until the next business day,
then contact support with the order id. We will not treat it as lost on the same day the
carrier marks it delivered.

## Addresses and failed delivery

- Delivery address can be changed only while the order is **placed** or **processing**.
  After **shipped**, we cannot redirect the carrier.
- We leave parcels in a safe place when no one is home. We do not require a signature.
- After two failed delivery attempts the parcel returns to us. Contact support; we can
  reship (you pay postage) or cancel and refund.

## What we ship

We ship the items on the order together. We do not split shipments.

Perishable pantry goods (syrup, tea) travel at ambient temperature. We do not offer
refrigerated delivery. In hot weather, syrup may thicken; that is not a defect.

## Coverage

We deliver to addresses in the same country as the shop. We do not ship internationally
and we do not hold orders for in-store pickup in this version.
